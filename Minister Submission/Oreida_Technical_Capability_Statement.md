## 1. Executive capability statement

Oreida Pvt Ltd is promoting the **Nzwisiso AI Policy Platform**, a browser-based capability that lets a government department test a draft policy against a simulated population before the policy is implemented, with **Edmore Zviitwah** providing business leadership and **Tadii Tendayi** providing technical leadership.

This statement complements — it does not duplicate — the principal proposal. Its purpose is to give the Government, and ZEPARI as a prospective partner, a concise, evidence-based view of the promoter's prior technical, digital-government, artificial-intelligence and delivery experience, so that the people promoting and technically leading the proposed platform can be assessed on their record rather than on assertion.

The experience most directly relevant to a policy-simulation platform for sixteen government institutions is the promoter's documented work in **digital-government readiness**, **data and artificial intelligence**, and **financial and policy analysis**, held as certificates with verifiable credential numbers and set out at Annex B. That record is complemented by blockchain and software-engineering pedigree through **cBLOCK** and **BitFlex**, by engagement with international technology and development ecosystems, and by leadership within the **United Africa Blockchain Association (UABA)**.

## 3. Government digital-transformation and policy experience

The promoter's **Digital Government Readiness Assessment** (credential number **0000066720-0000157752**) is directly on point for a platform intended to sit inside Government and to be assessed by a ministry's own advisers. It concerns how a public body establishes readiness for digital service delivery — the same question a Government appraiser will ask of this platform.

**Leading Equitable Organisations** and **Innovative Business Models for Better Impact** address how public-purpose institutions design programmes that reach people fairly and are structured to deliver measurable impact. A policy-simulation platform for sixteen institutions is judged on exactly those terms: whether the instrument improves the reach and the quality of a policy decision, and whether it is set up to be sustained.

This experience is a *capability* record, not a claim of prior Government deployment. **No claim is made in this statement that Oreida Pvt Ltd has itself delivered a national policy platform before.** The platform proposed is new; the capability to build, run and support it is what this statement evidences.

## 4. Artificial-intelligence and data capability

The promoter holds **Google AI for K12 Educators** (credential **438018998**) and **Intermediate use of Google Workspace for Education Fundamentals** (credential **437447288**), both issued by Google in November 2025 and valid to November 2035. These evidence working command of applied artificial intelligence in an educational and public-institution setting — the setting in which a policy-simulation capability is introduced to officers who are not technologists.

The platform's own design decision is that the parts written by artificial intelligence are produced by the **Nzwisiso AI Policy Simulation Engine™** over a defined interface, and that the same department and the same policy text always produce a byte-identical result. The capability statement supports that decision; the technical specification that follows it describes how it is met.

## 5. Software-engineering and blockchain pedigree

Tadii Tendayi's software-engineering record runs through **cBLOCK** (blockchain software development, a Startupbootcamp project) and **BitFlex** (a Zimbabwean digital-asset platform), supported by the **Diploma In Blockchain Technology And Applications** (credential **2097-18411115**), the **Diploma In Cryptocurrency** (credential **2373-18411115**) and **Cryptocurrency: Regulations, Trading And The Future** (credential **2376-18411115**).

The relevance to a policy platform is engineering discipline in systems that must be built to be trusted with real institutional use: deterministic behaviour, verifiable records, and separation of a demonstration from a production service. This is the discipline the proposal's three named build steps are designed to harden.
